03 workspace In about four minutes you have your own machine in the cloud. Your files, your agents, and a full workstation, right in the browser.
04 post Post a job. Here, appealing a denied MRI claim. That creates a durable case record on Terse and a channel on Agent Relay.

06 case The worker asks the bench. Policy, billing codes and appeals read the documents inside the VM, reply with citations, disagree, converge, and vote. Three of three.
07 approve The worker approves. The decision, the votes, and the exact agent sessions behind them are on the record forever.
08 supervisor If a payer goes quiet, the case wakes itself and escalates to the supervisor. Kill the server, restart, nothing is lost.

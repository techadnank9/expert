# Plan policy CG-0423.pdf (14 pages) — Harborline Health Clinical Guideline: Lumbar Spine MRI (pulled by Policy agent)
Section 1 Scope: CPT 72148, 72149, 72158.
Section 4.1 Red flags: MRI covered without conservative therapy when cancer history, infection, cauda equina, or trauma.
Section 4.2 Radiculopathy: MRI lumbar spine is considered medically necessary when ALL of: (a) radicular pain or radiculopathy present for at least 6 weeks; (b) documented failure of at least 6 weeks of conservative therapy, including physical therapy and/or NSAIDs; (c) the patient is a candidate for surgery or epidural injection and the result will change management. Progressive or new motor deficit satisfies 4.2(a) and (b) immediately.
Section 4.3 Not medically necessary: uncomplicated acute low back pain under 6 weeks without red flags.
Section 7 Documentation: submit the physician re-evaluation, therapy dates, and the imaging order with indication. Claims denied for missing documentation are overturned on Level 1 appeal when section 4.2 criteria are shown.
Effective Jan 1, 2026. Review: Jan 2027.
